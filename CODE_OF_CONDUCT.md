# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Beginner questions deserve patient, clear answers.

When participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces:

- Treat people with respect. Discuss the work and its effects without personal attacks.
- Explain technical concerns with examples, and accept corrections when evidence changes.
- Keep contributions relevant and give credit for others' work.
- Respect privacy. Do not post private messages, personal details, credentials, or account data without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, or post sexual content or spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or block accounts when needed to uphold these expectations. They will consider context, severity, and repeated behavior.

For a conduct concern, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls on the relevant content or account. Include enough context for review without publishing another person's private information. For a technical security vulnerability, use the separate [security-reporting route](SECURITY.md).

These expectations apply to maintainers as well as contributors and visitors.
